## Design Context

### Users
YouTubers creating football content. The context of use is as a tool to aid in creating visually appealing YouTube videos. The primary emotion the interface should evoke is "professional" and "tactical precision" suited for broadcast/video formats.

### Brand Personality
**Three Words**: Professional, Side-Project, Tactical. 
**Voice/Tone**: The interface should feel like a serious tool for football analysis, focusing on clarity and visual impact for video content creation.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Theme/Style**: A dark theme UI (already utilizing `#0f172a` and `#1e293b` backgrounds with `#10b981` accents). The focus is on a professional broadcast or tactical analysis tool look without relying on specific real-world brand references. The visuals prioritize legibility and contrast, crucial for being seen clearly on a YouTube video. 

### Design Principles
1. **Video-First Clarity**: All elements on the pitch and sidebar must be highly legible when downscaled or compressed in a YouTube video. High contrast and clean typography (Outfit font) are essential.
2. **Professional Polish**: It's a side project, but it must look like a premium broadcast tool. Use subtle gradients, distinct active states, and refined shadows (as already seen in the CSS tokens) to elevate the design.
3. **Unhindered Utility**: The interface should stay out of the way of the content (the pitch and players). Controls in the sidebar should be dense enough to be useful but clean enough not to distract.
4. **Smooth Interactions**: Given it's for video, animations (like the Progressive Reveal feature from previous conversations) should be smooth and deliberate, not jarring, aiding in storytelling.
